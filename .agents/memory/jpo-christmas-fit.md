---
name: Christmas fitting pitfalls
description: Native seasonal fitting needs pose anatomy and padded headwear, not just anchors.
---
Do not equate source layer classification with anatomy: Amelia's seated pink plaid can be classified as skin, and long rear hair can hide her entire shirt.

**Why:** Fitting clothing to layer bounds alone either left it unchanged or painted the wrong body area during visual QA.

**How to apply:** Inspect the base and split layers together. Protect actual face/hand colors and long hair; verify all characters and both seated halves. Tall hats need padded pose overlays because raised seated crowns leave too little room inside a 32px frame.

Inspect generated pet source sheets before assuming a fixed pose count.

**Why:** The adult reindeer source includes an extra walking pose; selecting only six connected components by area discarded the real sitting pose.

**How to apply:** Label every pose in the source, then compare the resulting walk/sit/action contact sheet in both directions.
