📣 Office Banner

Puts a message on EVERY screen until each person dismisses it. Posting a new one replaces the old one. Late logins see it too.

🎱 Office Bingo

Starts an office-wide bingo: 60s to join, the prize is paid by the house — no entry fee. Needs at least 2 players. Pick the win rule (any line, or BLACKOUT: fill the whole card), how balls get called (auto at your chosen pace, or you deal them like a real host — switchable mid-game).

🚦 Red Light, Green Light

Tournament rules: 45s to join, runners race the long floor while the giant doll turns around — move during RED LIGHT and you're OUT where you stand. Players can SHOVE each other (F key). Crossing the line only survives the ROUND: finishers race again (everyone else is out for good) until ONE champion takes the whole pot. Exception: if exactly 2 are left and both have survived 3 rounds, they split it. Nobody survives a round? Same field goes again. Few runners left = slippery floor. House pays, free to join, needs 3+.

💼 Deal or No Deal owner key only

The Vince Show, real-show rules: 16 briefcases, one holds the jackpot. The office votes cases open in rounds of 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1. After every round the Banker's phone rings — that's YOU: make a per-player offer (from the gold controls inside the game panel) or skip. They vote DEAL or NO DEAL (majority of all players). Ride to the last case and its value splits between everyone. House pays, free to join, 60s lobby, needs 2+ players.

🕒 Admin Activity Log

Everything done from this panel, newest first — who unlocked it, price changes (old → new), banners, bingo games, approvals, kicks, password resets. Individual bingo ball calls aren't listed to keep this readable. Written by the server, so it can't be edited from here.

Reset a Password

Clears the password for an email (works even if they're offline). They'll set a new one the next time they register with that email.

💰 Adjust Coins owner key only

Add or remove coins for any account (yours included). Positive adds, negative removes; balances never go below 0. Only visible when F8 is unlocked with the owner key.

🎁 Gift a Rare Pet owner key only

The Unicorn and Phoenix can't be bought — this is the ONLY way anyone gets one. Works even if they're offline; if they're online it appears at their feet instantly.

🙈 Hide & Seek (Prop Hunt)

Stand where everyone should gather (that's YOUR current spot), pick a disguise, then start. Joiners teleport to your spot and freeze while you hide; when the timer ends they hunt you at normal zoom. First one to press E on the right object wins.
